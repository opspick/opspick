Free booking tools and what they actually cost you

Free tiers are real. So are the three limits that show up in month two: staff, payments, and reminders.

What "free" usually means

ToolFree includesWhere it stops
FreshaCalendar, marketplace, clients, remindersPayments go through their processor
Square AppointmentsOne calendar, basic bookingSecond staff member, advanced reminders
Calendly (free)One event type, unlimited bookingsNo payments, no intake forms, no team routing
Google Calendar appointmentsSimple paid booking pageLimited customisation, few automations

The three limits that matter

1. The second chair

Most free tiers are single-calendar. The day you hire, you are on a paid plan. Budget for it before the hire, not after.

2. Payments

Free booking plus a separate card reader means two systems and manual reconciliation. Free booking with built-in payments means their processor rate, often 1.5-2.9%. On $8,000 a month, that is $120-230 — usually more than the "paid" plan you avoided.

3. Reminders and no-shows

Free tiers often include one reminder. One reminder is the setting that produces no-shows. If you cannot send a 48-hour and a 3-hour message, the free tool is costing you appointments.

When free is the right answer

  • You are pre-launch and testing demand.
  • You are solo with a simple 30-minute service.
  • You take payment in person and always have.

When to pay

Pay as soon as one of these is true: you hire, your no-show rate passes 10%, or you are reconciling payments by hand more than once a week. At that point $15-30 a month is not a cost, it is the cheapest fix available.

Practical path

Start free with Fresha if you need a marketplace and a calendar today. Move to Booksy when discovery is what limits growth. If you already take cards with Square, stay with Square Appointments and upgrade the plan the week you hire.
