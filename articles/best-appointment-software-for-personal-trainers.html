Options

Square Appointments

Good all-rounder: packages, prepayment and card on file in one system. Free tier is enough for a solo trainer with one calendar; add staff and you pay.

Booksy / Fresha

Built for salons. You can make them work, but packages and cancellation enforcement are clunkier than dedicated tools, and the consumer marketplace is not where fitness clients look.

Excellent booking link, weak payments and no package tracking. Fine if you invoice separately through Stripe.

The policy that changes your income

24-hour cancellation, charged in full, applied to everyone including friends. Announce it in writing at signup, put it in the confirmation message, and let the software charge â€” not you. Trainers who enforce this see fewer late cancellations within a month, because the rule is visible before it is tested.

Pricing sanity check

If you train 25 sessions a week at $60, one lost session a week costs $3,120 a year. A $30/month tool that prevents two a month pays for itself eight times over. Do not optimise this line item.

What I would do

Solo trainer: Square Appointments, packages + card on file, 24-hour policy in writing.

Online + in person mix: Calendly Pro for booking plus Stripe invoices, accept the seam.
