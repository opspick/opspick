The settings most shops get wrong

1. Deposits are off

Every tool ships with deposits disabled because it feels unfriendly. Turn them on for new clients only: 20-30% or a flat $10, refundable up to 24 hours. No-shows typically drop by half in the first month.

2. Reminders go out once, too early

One reminder 24 hours before is the default and the worst option. Use two: 48 hours (confirm or reschedule) and 3 hours (address, parking, "running late?"). Client replies to the first one are worth more than any loyalty program.

3. Booking window is open forever

Allow bookings 60 days out, not 6 months. Far-future bookings cancel more and clog the calendar.

4. No-shows are not tracked

Tag every no-show. After three, require a deposit. That single rule fixes most of the revenue leak without confrontation.

What I would do

Solo chair, first year: Fresha, deposits on for new clients, two reminders.

Two or more chairs with steady demand: Booksy, accept the fee, use the marketplace to fill slow days.

Already taking cards with Square: stay, upgrade the plan, do not add a second system.

Whatever you choose, spend your first week on the deposit policy, not on the logo.
