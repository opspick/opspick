Best booking app for mobile dog groomers

A mobile groomer is a route business wearing a grooming business as a costume. Most booking tools optimise for chairs, not vans.

Affiliate links below; pricing changes, verify before buying.

What a van actually needs

  1. Address on the booking — with a map link the client can confirm.
  2. Travel buffer between stops — 15-30 minutes depending on the city.
  3. Offline tolerance — you will lose signal in a driveway.
  4. Deposits — a no-show at a house costs you the drive, not just the slot.
  5. Photo history per pet — cuts the "what did we do last time" call.

Tool fit

Fresha

Free, works well for the calendar and deposits, weak on routing. You will keep a separate route plan. Fine for 1-3 dogs a day, painful at six.

Booksy

Marketplace discovery can fill gaps for a new mobile business, but the per-staff fee and chair-oriented flow mean you still plan the route elsewhere.

Square Appointments

Best if you already take card payments on the van. Location fields and client notes are usable; travel buffers are manual.

None of the three does routing well. Most mobile groomers run booking + a route app (or a shared calendar) and accept the seam.

Practical setup that works

Deposit at booking, always

Mobile no-shows are expensive: fuel, time and a slot you cannot refill. Charge a deposit for every new client and card-on-file for regulars.

Service windows, not exact times

Offer "morning (9-12)" and "afternoon (13-17)" windows. Clients accept it, and you stop lying about arrival times.

Text before you drive

An automated "on my way, 15 minutes" message cuts gate-code calls and no-answers dramatically.

What I would do

Start on Fresha (free) with deposits on, windows instead of exact slots, and a simple route plan in Google Maps. Move to Booksy only if you need the marketplace to find clients.
